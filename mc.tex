\section*{Abstract}
In this paper, we shall look at two state-of-the-art algorithms (in my humble opinion) for finding maximum clique in a finite graph. They use different characteristics of graph to find the clique, one uses k-core numbers and the other vertex colouring. I had implemented both algorithms and I shall present my findings about how well the implementation is feasible. I had implemented everything in C++17 and used a bit fancy techniques that I shall get into in this paper.
